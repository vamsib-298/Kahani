Request Account Deletion

We're sorry to see you go! If you would like to delete your Kahani account and all associated data, please follow the steps below:

Steps to delete:

  1. Send an email to support@kahani.app or dutypein@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include your registered Phone Number or Email address.

What happens next?

Once we receive your request, our team will verify your identity and permanently delete your profile, reading progress, and coin balance within 7 business days.

Note: This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
